Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2026

FrontFrame LLC respects your privacy. This policy describes how we handle information collected through our website.

AI Assistant

The chat widget on this site is an AI assistant, not a human. When you send a message, the text is processed by Anthropic (the company behind Claude) to generate a response. Anthropic receives your messages as part of this process. FrontFrame does not control Anthropic's internal data handling beyond what Anthropic's API terms provide. Do not share passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information in the chat.

Contact Information You Provide

If you share your name and contact details (email address or phone number) through the chat assistant or an intake form, that information is stored by FrontFrame and used only to follow up on your request. It is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS Notifications

When a visitor requests contact through the chat assistant, an automated SMS alert is sent to the FrontFrame business owner only. No visitor phone numbers are collected for outbound marketing. No marketing messages are sent to visitors.

Conversation Storage

FrontFrame may retain chat transcripts for internal quality review. Transcripts are not shared externally.

Client Communications

Clients engaged in an active project may receive SMS messages related to their engagement, including requests to review deliverables or respond to time-sensitive questions. These messages are transactional in nature and directly related to the services being performed under the client agreement. Consent to receive these messages is captured via initialed acknowledgment in the service agreement executed prior to engagement. Clients may opt out at any time by replying STOP.
