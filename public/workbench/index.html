Defining an Agent

A working examination of how a website assistant becomes tractable when its responsibilities are atomized into bounded agents, operational behaviors, and governed boundaries.

Study path

Encounter the problem → notice what deserves examination → examine it → reach a provisional understanding → carry a question forward.

Current working decision

Provisional
An FF agent is described by its purpose, scope, and required operational behaviors. The Constitution governs the exercise of those behaviors.

Operational primitives

01 Observe
02 Retrieve
03 Interpret
04 Recall
05 Deliberate
06 Commit
07 Generate
08 Modify
09 Persist
10 Execute

Working abstraction. Sufficient until evidence shows that it is not.

Boundary

Systems operations: what the agent can do.

Constitution: what the agent may do, when, and under whose authority.

Questions still open

Do all ten primitives survive testing against actual FF agents?
What evidence justifies creating an agent rather than a simpler component?
How should unsupported leaps be detected at agent handoffs?
Which controls are constitutional and which are operational?

Provenance

Derived from the design conversation that moved from cognitive limits of the whole assistant through atomization, agent behavior, operational primitives, and the systems/Constitution boundary. This page records a working state rather than a final specification.
