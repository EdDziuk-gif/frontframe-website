Approach Taken

Initial limits are 8 calls/minute per hashed IP and 100/hour site-wide and are explicitly provisional because production traffic is still too low to establish empirical limits. The resolve_gap work remains copy plus insertion-point documentation only; no dead conditional or partial Scoring Agent wiring was added.
