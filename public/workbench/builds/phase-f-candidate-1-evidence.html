What the accepted capability requires

  • Two tiers: Management (Operator + Delegates) and Staff. Assistant and outside resources contribute without organizational authority.
  • A durable proposal object. Authenticated Management/Staff and the trusted assistant path may persist proposals; outside resources contribute through authenticated Management/Staff.
  • Independent content origin and attribution: the server establishes submitted_by from trusted identity, ignoring client assertions, while source_type remains independently validated.
  • Operator-exclusive return, rejection, and immediate promulgation. Atomic amendment history, live provision update/create, proposal completion/linkage, and stale-text protection.
  • Denied actions leave protected objects unchanged. Equivalent open/reviewed incidents aggregate; recurrence after resolution creates a new incident. SMS is rate-limited per fingerprint and uses only fixed system-controlled content.
  • Operator audit visibility over proposals, promulgated amendments, and authorization incidents.
